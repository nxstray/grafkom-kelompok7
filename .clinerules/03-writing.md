# Writing Rules - Laporan LaTeX Kelompok 6

1. Language is Indonesian, human tone, short clear sentences.
2. Never use em dash or en dash. Use comma, period, or hyphen - only.
3. Format is LaTeX in docs/laporan.tex. Build with pdflatex or xelatex.
   Lab PCs have no LaTeX, so build the PDF with python docs/make_pdf.py.
4. Structure: cover, daftar isi, then one chapter per page.
5. Chapter title: centered, Times New Roman 14 pt, bold, starts new page.
6. Sub chapter: numbered, Times New Roman 12 pt, bold.
7. Body: Times New Roman 12 pt, justified, 1.5 line spacing.
8. Page number: centered in the footer. Cover and daftar isi have none.
9. Code snippets use English comments. Keep lines under 80 chars.
